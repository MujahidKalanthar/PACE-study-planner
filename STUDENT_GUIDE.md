# Study Planner — Get Started

> **Know what to study. Every day.**

Welcome to PACE! Setting up your study plan takes less than 5 minutes. Once configured, PACE calculates what you need to cover each day so you can study with calm consistency.

---

## 🚀 10-Step Quick Start Guide

### Step 1 — Create Your Account
1. Open the website.
2. Click **Sign Up** and enter your name, email, and password.
3. Check your email inbox for a verification link from Supabase.
4. Click the verification link, then return to the app and sign in.

---

### Step 2 — Set Up Your Target Exam
1. Enter what you are preparing for (e.g., **Class 11 Boards**, **Class 12 Boards**, **JEE Main**, **NEET UG**, **School Exams**, or custom targets).
2. Set your **Target Exam Date**.
3. Choose a color theme for your exam countdown.

---

### Step 3 — Add Your Syllabus
Choose the method that works best for you:

* **Option A: AI Syllabus Import (Fastest for PDFs & Scans)**
  - Upload a PDF, image scan, or paste your syllabus text.
  - The AI will automatically structure your document into **Subject → Chapter → Subtopic**.
  - Review and confirm the structured syllabus before adding it to your plan.

* **Option B: Starter Syllabi (Instant)**
  - Go to **Import → Starter Syllabi**.
  - Pick from pre-loaded syllabi:
    - **Class 11 CBSE (Science & Commerce)**
    - **Class 12 CBSE (Science)**
    - **JEE Main & Advanced (PCM)**
    - **NEET UG (Medical PCB)**
    - **Computer Science & GATE**
    - **Commerce & Economics**
  - Preview the chapters and click **Import Syllabus**.

* **Option C: Add Manually**
  - Create your own subjects, add chapters, set difficulty ratings (**Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**), and add subtopics.

---

### Step 4 — Set Your Study Availability & Timetable
1. Enter how many hours you want to study on standard days (e.g., 3 hours/day).
2. Go to **Settings → Weekly Timetable** to add fixed commitments like school hours or tuition classes.
3. The planner will automatically adjust available study time for each day of the week.

---

### Step 5 — Generate Your Daily Plan
1. Click **Generate Study Plan**.
2. The planner organizes your chapters into balanced daily study targets leading up to your exam.
3. Open **Your Plans** to see your daily schedule.

---

### Step 6 — Study Using Focus Mode
1. When you are ready to study, open today's target task and click **Start Focus Mode**.
2. Use the built-in timer to stay focused without distractions.
3. When finished, click **Complete Session**.
4. Log a quick rating (**Easy**, **Okay**, or **Hard**) to tell the planner how well you understood the concept.

---

### Step 7 — Let the Plan Adapt Automatically
- **Missed a study day?** Don't worry. Click **Re-balance Schedule** and PACE will gently redistribute remaining work across future days.
- **Finished early?** Mark your tasks completed, and the planner will recalculate your pace.
- **Spaced Revisions**: When you complete a chapter, PACE automatically schedules quick review sessions (1-day, 7-day, and 30-day reviews) so you don't forget past topics.

---

### Step 8 — Track Your Progress
Open the **Progress** tab to view:
- **Study Streak**: Days in a row you've completed study sessions.
- **Total Hours Studied**: Total time invested in your preparation.
- **Subject Mastery**: Percentage completion for each subject.
- **Upcoming Revisions**: Revision tasks due soon.

---

### Step 9 — Connect with Study Buddies
1. Go to the **Friends** tab.
2. Type a classmate's registered name or email in the search bar.
3. Click **Send Request**.
4. Once they accept, you will see each other in your **Study Circle**.
5. Tap **Send Nudge** to encourage each other to keep studying!
6. *Privacy Note*: You can toggle whether your study statistics are visible to friends anytime in Settings.

---

### Step 10 — Customize Your Experience
Open **Settings** (More) to:
- Toggle between **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**.
- Adjust daily target study hours.
- Update profile details or change notification settings.
- Submit feedback or report any issues directly to our team.
